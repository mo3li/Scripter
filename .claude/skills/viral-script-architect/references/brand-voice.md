## 3. Speaking Egyptian to a Gulf audience

Default rules until his answers refine them:
- **Keep his Egyptian voice.** Don't switch to Gulf dialect and never imitate or mock a Gulf accent. Gulf viewers understand mainstream Egyptian well; the problem is only deep local slang and Egypt-only references.
- **Choose widely understood words.** Prefer the common Egyptian word over street slang that doesn't travel (e.g. "يسطا", "فكك", "اشمعنى", "لبّس", "هبّل"). If a word might not land, use the plainer one.
- **Examples and references from their world.** Swap Egypt-only references (the pound, Cairo metro, كشري, الثانوية العامة, local brands and places) for ones a Gulf viewer lives with, or for neutral ones. Don't stuff in landmarks for show.
- **Currency.** Use the target country's currency (درهم / ريال سعودي / ريال قطري). When a video speaks to all three, use percentages, a neutral phrasing, or USD. Preferred default: {…}
- **Respect.** Nothing that jokes about Gulf culture, religion, rulers or nationalities. Topics he wants to avoid: {…}
