# Niche Positioning for Brokers: own one price band

Source: the user's file «اختر نطاق سعري واحد وتملّكه» ("pick your price band"). Use it when:
- planning content for a broker or brokerage
- writing a bio
- the user asks what to talk about or who the content is for

## The idea

- **"A broker in Dubai" is forgettable. "The 1–3M guy in JVC" gets the first call.** When you're for everyone, nobody remembers you.
- The specific beats the big, every time. People mention a specific broker in comments and conversations, and recommend them by name.
- **Positioning compounds like rent, not like a lottery ticket.** Give a band 90 days of content before judging it.

## Picking the band: 4 lenses (one night, four honest questions)

Circle one answer per lens. If the answers agree, that's the band. If they disagree, lens 1 wins, because closed deals beat opinions. If it's a tie, take the deepest pool in the market: off-plan, 1–3M AED.

| # | Lens | The question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your deals** | Last 12 months: how many deals did you close at 1–3M? 3–5M? 5–8M? | Deals don't lie; feelings do. Your history shows where you already win. |
| 2 | **Your network** | What can your circle (friends, colleagues, family who trust you) realistically buy in 1–2 years? | Your next ten deals come from people who already trust you. |
| 3 | **Your city** | In your main area, which band has many good buildings, real demand (families or investors) and reasonable yearly costs? | A band needs depth, not one famous tower. |
| 4 | **Your energy** | Do you enjoy teaching first-time buyers the basics (mostly 1–3M), or talking numbers with experienced investors (mostly 3–8M)? | You can't fake interest for 90 days of content. |

## Market numbers (from the file; check against current DLD data before quoting on camera)

The file cites DLD figures and published market reports for 2023–2025, rounded:

| Band (AED) | ~Deals in 2023 (of ~115k) | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| < 1M | ~35k | High volume, small commissions, fierce price competition |
| 1–2M | ~40k | The biggest single band, about a third of the market. Mid-market flats and townhouses, first-time buyers, yield investors |
| 2–5M | ~27k | Townhouses and family villas, upsizers, golden-visa buyers (above 2M) |
| 5–10M | ~6k | Prime. Fewer deals, longer cycles, all relationships |
| 10M+ | ~3.5k | Ultra-luxury. Big headlines, but a small pool dominated by established names |

- About half of all deals fall between 1M and 3M.
- Off-plan share of sales: ~40% (2020), ~52% (2023), ~72% (late 2024).
- At a 2% commission, one 1–3M deal pays about AED 20k–60k.
- The file claims ~29,600 registered brokers in mid-2025, averaging ~1.4 deals each per 6 months. Its conclusion: "the market isn't short of deals; it's short of brokers who own a clear lane."

Truth rule: these are the file's rounded figures. In a script, either attribute them ("حسب أرقام دائرة الأراضي لسنة ٢٠٢٣ تقريبًا…") or verify them first. Never present them as exact or current.

## After choosing: what changes

- **Positioning line:** «أساعد الناس يشتروا بشكل أساسي بين [X] و[Y] درهم في [المنطقة]».
- **Bio:** points to the main band the same day.
- **Content:** every video speaks to *that* buyer: their fears, their numbers, their areas. Deals outside the band are fine to take, but don't post about them.
- **Ideas get easier:** one buyer, one set of fears, endless specific topics:
  - buildings
  - service charges
  - traps
  - payment plans
  - first-timer questions
- **Closing gets easier:** you know the buildings, fees and traps by heart.

## 3 numbers to track weekly (try to beat your own record)

1. Deals inside the band vs outside it.
2. Messages that name your band or area.
3. People introducing you by your band. This is reputation you hear with your own ears.

## How the skill uses this

- **Ideas and content plans:** if the band and area aren't known, ask once. Otherwise build the ideas around that buyer, using the 4 lenses as discovery questions (`content-system.md` §7a).
- **Hooks:** speak to the band buyer directly, e.g. «لو ميزانيتك بين مليون و٣ مليون في دبي…».
- **Bios and CTAs:** carry the positioning line.
- **Testimonials:** the file's client stories are the author's marketing claims. Never reuse them as facts in the user's scripts.
