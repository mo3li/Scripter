# Storytelling, Educational and Selling Structures

Fill-in templates for scripts that tell a story, teach, or sell. Pick one per video. Every story beat must be true. If the user has no real story for a slot, ask for one or use a structure that doesn't need it.

## Part A: Story structures

### 1. Hero's journey (the creator or a client is the hero)
1. **Intro**: the hero and the problem. *"من [مدة] كنت [المشكلة]…"*
2. **Inflection point**: the pain the problem caused. *"وبسبب ده كنت [الأعراض/الخسارة]…"*
3. **Rising action**: the failed attempts. *"جربت [حل ١] و[حل ٢]… ومفيش فايدة."*
4. **Climax**: the solution. *"لحد ما بعد [مدة] لقيت الحاجة الوحيدة اللي نفعت: [الحل]."*
5. **Falling action**: the results. *"أول ما بدأت أعمل كده، [النتيجة/الدليل]."*
6. **Resolution + CTA**: the business or service. *"وعشان كده بدأت [الخدمة] عشان أساعد ناس زيي يوصلوا لـ [النتيجة]."*

### 2. About me (personal-brand intro)
1. **Intro**: normal starting point. *"أنا [الاسم]، ومن [مدة] كنت شخص عادي بشتغل [الشغل]…"*
2. **Conflict**: the event that changed things. *"لحد ما [الحدث]."*
3. **Epiphany**: the realisation. *"ساعتها فهمت إن [الإدراك]."*
4. **Change**: what you did, where you are now. *"فعملت [الفعل]… ودلوقتي [النتيجة]."*
5. **Purpose**: the deeper why. *"[النتيجة] غيّرت حياتي، وبقت مهمتي أساعد [الجمهور] ينتقلوا من [المشكلة] لـ [النتيجة]."*

### 3. The lesson
1. **Setback**: a bold, surprising hook. *"أنا [حدث صادم]."*
2. **Pain**: what you tried. *"جربت [١] و[٢] و[٣] ومفيش نتيجة، فبدأت [الفعل]."*
3. **Resolution**: the result. *"وفي خلال [مدة] [النتيجة]."*
4. **Lesson**: one sentence. *"الموقف ده علّمني إن [الدرس]."*

### 4. The big goal / dream (series starter)
1. **The dream**: where it comes from. *"من وأنا عندي [سن] وأنا نفسي [الحلم]…"*
2. **Pursuit**: the moment you started. *"لحد ما في يوم [الحدث]، وقررت إنها دلوقتي أو مفيش. هكون صريح، كنت [خايف/متوتر] بس عملتها."*
3. **Progress update**: where you are. *"لحد دلوقتي عملت [خطوات]، وداخل على [الخطوة الجاية]. عشان أوصل لـ [الحلم] قبل [تاريخ] لازم أخلّص ده في [مدة]."*
4. **CTA**: invite them along. *"عايز تشوف هلحق ولا لأ؟ تابعني."*

### 5. Challenge to victory
1. **Doubt**: *"من [مدة] حد قالي إني عمري ما هوصل لـ [النتيجة]."*
2. **Struggle**: *"كنت بعمل [فعل] ولسه بجيب [نتيجة وحشة]."*
3. **Turning point**: *"لحد ما فهمت [الإدراك]."*
4. **Solution**: a named method or numbered steps. *"مشيت على طريقة من ٣ خطوات: …"*
5. **Transformation**: *"انتقلت من [قبل] لـ [بعد]، وكمان [أثر جانبي حلو]."*
6. **Myth bust**: *"[النتيجة] مش صعبة، إنت بس غالبًا بتعمل الحاجات الغلط."*

### 6. The breakthrough
1. **Problem**: *"كنت بعمل [الحل] ولسه [المشكلة]، وحتى لما جربت [حاجات تانية] فضلت [المشكلة]."*
2. **Breakthrough**: *"لحد ما اكتشفت [إدراك]"* or *"لحد ما عملت حاجة واحدة جابتلي [نتيجة]."*
3. **Fix / takeaway**: how you found it and the steps. *"الخطوة الأولى… بعدها… وآخر حاجة…"*

## Part B: Educational structures

| Structure | Beats |
|---|---|
| **Tip / hack** | Hook (valuable info coming) → the tip in one line (skip if the hook said it) → how-to steps → CTA (follow for more) |
| **Myth** | Hook → state the myth → debunk it (why it's wrong) → what to do instead → subtle promote → CTA |
| **Viral video reaction** | 5–7s clip of a viral video in the niche → expert opinion (true? why; or wrong? what to do instead) → CTA (follow the expert) |
| **Step-by-step system** | Hook → "here are the X steps" (name the framework if there is one) → step 1–3, each in 1–2 short sentences → optional promote → CTA |
| **Common mistake** | Hook → the mistake → why it's a mistake → what to do instead → optional promote → CTA |
| **Authority** | Hook → social proof (real result for you or a client) → how-to → CTA |

## Part C: Selling structures

### Selling a product or service
1. **Hook**: a common pain of the ideal client.
2. **Solution exists**: "there's a way out, you don't have to feel like this."
3. **Value**: an actionable, step-by-step fix they can use even without buying.
4. **Promote**: subtly, your product/service does exactly this.
5. **CTA**: sales CTA (DM, link, keyword).

### The 4 questions every buyer answers first
An offer answers one question. Stories answer four. Before anyone pays, they silently answer:

| The buyer's question | The story that answers it |
|---|---|
| Why should I trust you? | Your own story: what you tried and learned before reaching your method |
| Why will this help me? | Your method story: how you work, and why in this order |
| Why are you right for me? | A client like them: their situation before and what changed after |
| Why believe you'll get me a result? | A documented result: a case or a number you can prove |

Any question left unanswered comes back as "I'll think about it". One story per question is enough for a month of content: each story becomes a post, a short video and a story.

### The 9-step agreement ladder (sequencing content that sells)
People buy after reaching nine internal agreements, in order. Each piece of content should create one of them:

| # | What the client says to themselves | Content that creates it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I can't keep ignoring this problem | Names the problem precisely and shows what it costs today |
| 2 | My current way won't get me there | Shows the ceiling of the method they use now |
| 3 | There's a cause I don't know, and I need a real fix | Reveals the real cause behind the problem |
| 4 | This person understands me | Your story, or someone who went through what they're going through |
| 5 | This is new to me, and it could actually work | Explains your method and gives it a name |
| 6 | This is real: real people, real results | A detailed case study or documented result |
| 7 | Someone in my exact situation did it, so I can | A client story matching their level and circumstances |
| 8 | Staying as I am costs more than the solution | Makes them calculate the cost of another month the same way |
| 9 | Now, because waiting makes it heavier | Gives an honest reason to act today |

Use this ladder when the user asks for a content plan, a series, or a sales campaign: map each video to one rung, in order. The sale then becomes a natural last step.

Truth rule: steps 6–9 must use real results and honest urgency. Never invent case studies, deadlines or scarcity.

### Why stories sell now, and the order that makes it work
Trust online is low: anyone can claim anything, and many people have bought bad services before. So people guard their decisions and avoid anyone who feels like they're selling. They still want to buy, because the need is real. Stories do the selling that pitches can't: an offer answers one question, while four stories answer the four buyer questions above. Any question left unanswered comes back as "I'll think about it".

Build in this order. Each layer rests on the one before:
1. **Message**: what the creator truly believes, expressed through their work and content.
2. **Offer**: a service with a real result, its value laid out clearly (a presentation or proposal).
3. **Content**: the creator's stories and clients' stories, tied to their values, philosophy and principles. For Mohamed, that's faith and ambition together (see `brand-voice.md`).
4. **Sale**: done on principles and values, at a price that matches the value. Belief is built before the call, so the call confirms instead of convincing.

When writing a sales or BOF script, check which layer is missing. Content can't fix a missing message or a vague offer. Say so instead of writing a harder pitch.
