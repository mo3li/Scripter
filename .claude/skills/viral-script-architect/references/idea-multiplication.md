# One Viral Idea → 70 Videos

Use this when the user asks for content ideas, a content calendar, "more videos from this idea", or brings a viral video they want to build on.

## Step 1: Find a proven topic (the 5X rule)

A video is a real outlier when its **views are at least 5× the creator's follower count**. That means the topic travelled beyond the creator's own audience, so the topic itself is proven, not just the account.

Ask the user for the video's views and the creator's followers when possible. If they can't provide them, say the topic is unproven and continue anyway.

## Step 2: Write 7 angles for the topic

Take the core topic (e.g. "buying off-plan in Dubai") and write one video idea per angle:

| # | Angle | Pattern | Example (off-plan in Dubai) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Challenge** | Is it possible to [result] in [time]? | ينفع تشتري وحدة في دبي بـ [مبلغ] بس؟ |
| 2 | **Step-by-step tutorial** | If I had to [result] in [time], here are the X things I'd do | لو هشتري أول وحدة أوف بلان النهارده، دي الـ ٥ خطوات اللي هعملها |
| 3 | **Comparison** | Person A did X, person B did Y. Who won? | واحد اشترى جاهز وواحد اشترى أوف بلان بنفس المبلغ. مين كسب؟ |
| 4 | **Transformation** | [time] ago I set out to [result], here's what happened | *(real story only)* |
| 5 | **Educational** | Here's everything you need to know about [topic] | كل اللي محتاج تعرفه عن نسبة السداد قبل البيع |
| 6 | **Myth / common mistake** | NEVER do X if you want Y | أبدًا متحجزش من غير ما تسأل على حساب الضمان |
| 7 | **Storytelling** | I went from X to Y, here are my 3 tips | *(real story only)* |

Angles 4 and 7 need real personal experience. If the user has none, replace them with a client story they're allowed to share, or skip them.

## Step 3: Film each angle in 10 formats

The same script can be filmed 10 different ways:

1. **Talking back & forth**: the creator plays two characters, or two people talk.
2. **Voiceover**: narration over footage.
3. **Trending audio + B-roll**: text on screen, trending sound, no talking.
4. **Talking head**: straight to camera.
5. **Reaction / stitch**: react to another video or a comment.
6. **Shot / angle changes**: talking head with a cut on every line.
7. **Visuals**: graphics, screenshots, documents, numbers on screen.
8. **Green / split screen**: the creator in front of a screenshot, article or listing.
9. **Interview**: ask someone (a client, an expert, a person on the street).
10. **Clone**: the creator appears twice and argues both sides.

**7 angles × 10 formats = 70 videos from one proven topic.**

## How to deliver

- For an ideas request: give a table of the 7 angles with one hook each, and the best 2–3 formats for each angle. Don't write 70 full scripts unless asked.
- For a script request on one angle: write the script (normal rules, §22 of SKILL.md), and note which format it's written for.
- Different formats need different scripts. Trending-audio and visual formats need on-screen text, not spoken lines. Talking back & forth needs dialogue with speaker labels.
