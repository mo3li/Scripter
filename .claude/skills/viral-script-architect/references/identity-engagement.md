# Identity Engagement: content people engage with to say something about themselves

People often like, save, share or comment not only because the content is useful, but because **engaging says something about who they are**. A like is public. A share is a message. A comment is a statement. When a post lets someone show others "this is me", "I think like this", or "I belong to the smart ones", they engage to be seen engaging.

Example: a post says «انت مش مشتت… انت متعدد المهارات», with the caption «استخدمها صح وطورها». The viewer likes it because it describes them in a flattering way, and because they'd be happy for others to see them agree with it.

The goal for our brand is that engaging with Mohamed's content feels like joining **a community that thinks at a higher level**: long-term, informed, ambitious, principled.

## The test (run on every hook and caption)

> Would the viewer be **proud** to have this post appear under their name, in their story, or sent from their account?

If yes, it earns identity engagement. If it's just information, add an identity layer (below) or accept that it's a pure-value video.

## 7 mechanisms

| # | Mechanism | What the viewer signals | Pattern | Example in our niche |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flattering reframe** | "My 'flaw' is actually a strength" | انت مش [صفة سلبية]… انت [نفس الصفة بشكل راقي] | «انت مش متردد… انت بتدرس قبل ما تدخل» |
| 2 | **The tribe line** | "I'm one of the few who get it" | في ناس بتـ[X]… وفي ناس بتـ[Y] | «في ناس بتسأل السوق هيقع؟ وفي ناس بتسأل أدخل إزاي صح؟» |
| 3 | **Manifesto** | "This is what I believe" | a short, confident belief statement | «الفلوس الحلال اللي تكفي أهلك مش ماديّة… دي مسؤولية» |
| 4 | **Quiet flex** | "I have taste and knowledge" | لو انت بتعمل [تصرف ذكي]… انت مش [عادي] | «لو بتسأل عن حساب الضمان قبل السعر… انت مش مشتري عادي» |
| 5 | **Share as a message** | "I'm telling you something through this" | ابعتها لـ[شخص محدد] | «ابعتها لصاحبك اللي بقاله سنتين بيقول هشتري السنة الجاية» |
| 6 | **Future self** | "This is who I'm becoming" | اللي [هدف] مش بيعمل [X]… بيعمل [Y] | «اللي عايز يملك أصل قبل الأربعين مش بيستنى الوقت المثالي» |
| 7 | **Values** | "I'm principled and ambitious at the same time" | combines faith or ethics with success | «دنيا وآخرة مع بعض… مش لازم تختار» |

Combine 1–2 per script. Mechanism 2 (tribe) is the core of "a community that thinks at a higher level".

## How to write it

- **Speak to the viewer as the elevated identity** ("انت من الناس اللي…"). They should recognize themselves, not be lectured.
- **Name the tribe.** Give the community a recurring name or phrase the account owns (e.g. "اللي بيفكر بعقل المستثمر"), and reuse it across videos so followers adopt it.
- **The hook is a label, the body is the proof.** After the flattering line, give real value that justifies it. Otherwise it's empty flattery and the smart audience leaves.
- **The caption carries the identity line.** A short statement they'd agree with publicly (e.g. «استخدمها صح وطوّرها»), not a summary of the video.
- **Identity CTAs**, one per video. Only use one that fits:
  - comment a word that states identity: «لو انت من الناس دي اكتب "أنا"»
  - share to a specific person: «ابعتها لـ…»
  - save as a personal commitment: «احفظها عشان ترجعلها قبل أول قرار»
  - a question whose answer shows their thinking: «انت من أنهي نوع؟ ١ ولا ٢؟»

## Guardrails

- **Elevated, not arrogant.** Lift the viewer up; don't put other people down. "We think long-term" is fine; "everyone else is stupid" or mocking specific groups, nationalities or beliefs is not. Contempt drives away the classy audience we want and breaks the brand rules in `brand-voice.md`.
- **Honest flattery only.** The reframe must be genuinely true (hesitation *can* be diligence). No fake exclusivity ("only 1% will understand"), no invented statistics.
- **No promises of wealth or results.** Identity content sells a way of thinking, not guaranteed money.
- **Religion and values:** sincere and respectful, never used as a sales trick.
- **Not every video.** Mix identity posts with pure-value, story and sales content. If every post flatters, it loses weight.

## Where it fits

- Mostly top of funnel (reach and follows) and trust-building.
- It pairs well with the tribe/ladder steps "this person understands me" and "someone like me did it" in `storytelling-structures.md`.
