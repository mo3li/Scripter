# Hook Bank

Fill-in-the-blank hook templates, grouped by mechanism, plus visual hook formats and patterns from high-view reels. Use them as starting points, never as finished lines. Always adapt them to the creator's niche, voice and real facts.

Placeholders:
- **[الجمهور]**: who the video is for.
- **[النتيجة]**: the result they want.
- **[المشكلة]**: a common pain point they have.
- **[المجال]**: something specific to the niche that the audience recognises.
- **[رقم] / [مدة]**: real numbers and time frames only (see the truth rules below).

## Truth rules for templates

- A template with a number or a result (`X%`, `in 30 days`, `I made X`) may only be used when the creator has the real figure. Otherwise, pick a template without a number, or leave `[رقم]` for the user to fill.
- Never invent statistics to fill a template, even when a hook guide says "made-up numbers work". A made-up number is a lie to the viewer.
- Tension words (خطير، سري، "حاسس إنه مش قانوني") are for videos that really deliver something surprising. Overusing them reads as clickbait.
- Talking about yourself (my result, my story) works best once the audience already trusts you. For a new account, lead with value for the viewer.

## 1. Question hooks

A question makes the viewer answer it in their head, which buys a few seconds of attention.

| Template | Egyptian example |
|---|---|
| تخيل لو تقدر [النتيجة] | تخيل لو تقدر تشتري شقتك من غير ما تدفع مقدم كبير |
| ليه محدش بيتكلم عن [المجال]؟ | ليه محدش بيتكلم عن بند التنازل في عقود الأوف بلان؟ |
| طب لو قلتلك فيه طريقة أسهل لـ [النتيجة]؟ | طب لو قلتلك فيه طريقة أسهل تعرف السعر الحقيقي للشقة؟ |
| ولو تقدر [النتيجة] من غير [المشكلة]؟ | ولو تقدر تستثمر في عقار من غير ما تقفل فلوسك سنين؟ |
| بتسأل نفسك ليه [المشكلة]؟ | بتسأل نفسك ليه الشقة اللي جنبك اتباعت بأغلى منك؟ |
| بتعاني من [المشكلة]؟ جرب ده | بتعاني إن محدش بيكمّل الفيديو بتاعك؟ جرب ده |
| ينفع حد مبتدئ خالص [النتيجة]؟ | ينفع حد مبتدئ خالص يعمل أول استثمار عقاري؟ |
| ينفع فعلًا [النتيجة] في [مدة]؟ | ينفع فعلًا تبيع شقتك في أسبوع؟ |
| إيه اللي بيحصل لما [فعل]؟ | إيه اللي بيحصل لما تتأخر قسط واحد؟ |
| عمرك [المشكلة]؟ | عمرك مضيت على حاجة من غير ما تقراها؟ |

## 2. Number and list hooks

People save lists, and a number promises a clear, finite payoff.

| Template | Egyptian example |
|---|---|
| [رقم] خطوات لـ [النتيجة] | ٣ خطوات قبل ما تحجز أي وحدة |
| [رقم] حاجات عن [المجال] كنت أتمنى أعرفها من الأول | ٣ حاجات عن الأوف بلان كنت أتمنى أعرفها من الأول |
| [رقم] علامات إن [المشكلة] | ٥ علامات إن المطور ده هيتأخر في التسليم |
| [رقم] أخطاء بتعملها لو مش [النتيجة] | ٥ أخطاء بتعملها لو المحتوى بتاعك مش بيبيع |
| [رقم] حيل محدش بيقدّرها لـ [النتيجة] | ٣ حيل محدش بيقدّرها في التفاوض على السعر |
| الغلطة دي ممكن تكلفك [رقم] | الغلطة دي ممكن تكلفك [رقم] ألف درهم |
| ده السبب إن [رقم]% من [الجمهور] [المشكلة] | *(only with a real, sourced percentage)* |

|---|---|

## 7. Visual and format hooks (the first frame, not the words)

A strong opening shot can be the hook by itself, or can stack with a spoken line.

| Format | What happens on screen |
|---|---|
| **Setup vs result** | Show the finished result first, then cut back to how it started |
| **Match cut** | A movement in one shot continues into the next scene |
| **Clone** | The creator appears twice and argues or talks to themselves |
| **Bounce-in** | The creator jumps or pops into frame on the first word |
| **Running to camera** | The creator walks or runs into a close-up while talking |
| **Car hook** | Talking-head filmed in the car. Feels casual and private |
| **Busy day** | Quick cuts of a real day, with the voiceover on top |
| **Paper / caption reveal** | The hook or result is written on paper and shown to camera |
| **Reaction / stitch** | React to another video or a comment on screen |
| **Compare and contrast** | Two options side by side, like two units or two prices |
| **"Guess the price"** | Show the property or product and ask the viewer to guess |
| **POV / "When someone asks…"** | Relatable situation in text, creator acts it out |
| **Straight to the point** | No intro; the first words are the answer |
| **Creative angle** | An unusual camera position (floor, through an object, top-down) |
| **"Day 1 of…" / series** | Opens a series, which gives the viewer a reason to follow |

## 8. Patterns from ~2,000 high-view reels (10 niches)

The spreadsheet covered Fitness, Travel, Beauty, Food, Business, Photography, Fashion, Real Estate, Personal Development and Social Media. Most hooks were over 1M views. These are observations, not guarantees. Views depend on the video, the account and timing, not only the hook.

- **Short wins.** Most top hooks are 3–10 words ("Whipped Coffee", "Which shoe?", "What people think burns fat:").
- **Relatable POV beats information.** "When someone asks you how you got…", "POV:", "Me at 10 imagining…" appear again and again near the top.
- **Specific money and objects.** "a 5 bedroom house for $250,000", "a mistake that would cost me over $95,000". Real, concrete figures pull curiosity.
- **Conflict and drama.** "First reaction evicting my tenant from HELL" was the top real-estate hook. It works because it is a real story with stakes.
- **Negative commands.** "NEVER put your home in your will", "Stop doing this". They are short, urgent and useful.
- **Games and guesses.** "Guess the price of this penthouse", "Smart or just stubborn?", "Which shoe?" get the viewer to answer and comment.
- **Unfinished sentences.** Hooks ending in ":" or "…" ("What people think burns fat:") force the viewer to wait for the reveal.
- **Arabic hooks (a ~500-hook Arabic sheet, mostly Business, Personal Development and Social Media, in Gulf dialect).** The same patterns hold: short relatable situations ("موظفين انمسكوا يقدموا على وظائف وقت الدوام"), "why doesn't school teach this?" ("ليش ما يدرسوا هذا بالمدارس؟"), bold warnings ("لا تشتغل بمشروع مع أصحابك") and before/after ("مشروعي قبل سنتين"). When adapting to Egyptian, keep the mechanism, change the dialect.
- **Real estate specifically.** The top performers were drama (tenants, closing day), price reveals and guessing games, "buy X and live in it" strategy, and inheritance or tax warnings. Plain listing tours rank lower unless there is a twist ("Do you believe this is a New York City apartment?").

## How to use this bank

1. Pick 2–3 mechanisms that fit the idea and the objective (§9 of SKILL.md).
2. Fill each template with the creator's real niche, audience and facts.
3. Rewrite it in their voice. The final hook should not look like a template.
4. Pair the spoken hook with a visual hook from §7 when it helps.
5. Deliver the best one in the script, and 2 alternatives labeled by mechanism.
